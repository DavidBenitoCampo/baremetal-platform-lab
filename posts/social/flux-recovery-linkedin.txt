I deliberately broke a Kubernetes deployment to see how it would recover.

I'm running K3s on a Raspberry Pi 5, with Flux managing the application from Git.

For this test, I changed Podinfo's readiness probe to an unused port. The new Pod stayed unready, while the two previous Pods remained Ready. Flux reported a failed health check.

I reverted the change in Git. Eventually both the Deployment and Flux reported a healthy state.

But the HTTP recordings showed something I couldn't explain.

During the failed release, the probe recorded 1,800 HTTP 200 responses and no timeouts.

In the recovery capture, it recorded:
- 1,194 HTTP 200 responses
- 6 consecutive timeouts

The timeouts started roughly three minutes after the Deployment had reported recovery.

I don't know yet whether the issue was in the application, Ingress, SSH tunnel, or somewhere else in the request path. The probe ran from my workstation through a tunnel, so a healthy Deployment and a failed HTTP request don't necessarily contradict each other.

Next time I'll keep one uninterrupted capture running across the whole experiment and collect matching Ingress and host logs.

I'm keeping the evidence, including the things I haven't figured out yet:
