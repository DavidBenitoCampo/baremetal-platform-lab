I'm building a Kubernetes platform on a Raspberry Pi 5 to explore GitOps, observability, and what happens when infrastructure fails.

I've spent the past few years working with Linux systems, networks, and infrastructure. I wanted to take some of that experience further by building and operating my own environment.

What started as a Raspberry Pi running Ubuntu has grown into a small Kubernetes homelab.

My current setup includes K3s, Flux, Ansible, Prometheus, Grafana, GitHub Actions, and Ollama for local AI inference.

But the interesting part for me isn't getting everything running.

It's understanding what happens when something breaks.

What happens when a Kubernetes deployment fails? How does Flux respond? What do the metrics tell me? How do I know whether a service stayed available during recovery?

I'm using this project to explore those questions, test different approaches, and document the results, including the things that don't go as planned.

I'll be sharing what I build, the problems I investigate, and the engineering decisions behind them.

If you're working with Linux, DevOps, SRE, or Platform Engineering, I'd love to exchange ideas.
