I've spent the past few years working with Linux systems and infrastructure. Recently, I started building a small platform at home to explore the engineering behind reliable services.

The hardware is a Raspberry Pi 5 with an SSD. The software has grown into a single-node Kubernetes homelab with K3s, Flux, Ansible, Prometheus, Grafana, and Ollama.

I'm interested in the operational questions: What happens when a rollout fails? How do I spot the failure? Does a healthy Deployment mean the service was available? What evidence do I have after recovery?

I'll share the experiments, mistakes, measurements, and trade-offs as I work through them. Not every test ends with a clean result, and that's part of the point.

If you work with Linux, DevOps, SRE, or platform engineering, I'd enjoy comparing approaches.

Repository: https://github.com/DavidBenitoCampo/baremetal-platform-lab
